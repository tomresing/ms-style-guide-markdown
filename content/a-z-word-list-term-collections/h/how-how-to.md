## how to, how-to

Don’t use *how-to* as a noun. Always hyphenate as an adjective. Never capitalize the *t* in *to*.

**Examples**  
how-to book  
how-to article  
How to add an article to the library  
(Title-style capitalization) Writing a How-to Article
