## allow list

An *allow list* is a list of users, accounts, or other entities allowed to access resources and other things. It's the opposite of a *block list*.

Guidelines:

- Two words.
- Lowercase.
- Don't use as a verb, such as *allow-list the address*. Use phrasing like *add to an allow list* or *use an allow list*.
- Don't use one word (*allowlist*).
