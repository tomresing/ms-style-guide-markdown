## block list

A *block list* is a list of users, accounts, or other entities restricted from accessing resources and other things. It's the opposite of an *allow list*.

Guidelines:

- Two words.
- Lowercase.
- Don't use as a verb, such as *block-list the address*. Use phrasing like *add to a block list* or *use a block list*.
- Don't use one word (*blocklist*).
