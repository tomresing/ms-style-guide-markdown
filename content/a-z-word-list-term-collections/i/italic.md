## italic

Use only as an adjective, not as a noun. Don't use *italics* or *italicized*.

**Examples**  
To make the selected characters italic, select **Ctrl+I**.  
The characters are displayed in italic text.

In writing for customers, use *regular font* to describe text that's neither bold nor italic.
