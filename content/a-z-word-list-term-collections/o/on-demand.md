The term *on demand* describes something that's available or provided whenever it's requested or needed.

Don't hyphenate *on demand* when you're using it as an adverbial phrase after a verb.

**Examples**  
Watch on demand.  
Provision your resources on demand.  
You can access reports on demand from the dashboard.

Hyphenate *on-demand* when you're using it as a adjective that comes before a noun.

**Examples**  
Watch the on-demand webinar.  
Use on-demand compute resources to scale quickly.  
The service provides on-demand reporting.
