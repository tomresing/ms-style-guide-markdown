## opt in, opt out

*Opt in* is a general term for an action that a user takes to subscribe to, sign up for, or start to receive information about a service, app, or site, or to give consent for data collection, processing, and storage.

*Opt out* is a general term for an action that a user takes to avoid subscribing, signing up, or giving consent, or to unsubscribe, cancel a sign-up, or withdraw consent.

Unless space is limited or you're referring to the opt-in or opt-out process in a general way, use a more precise term that gives a clearer idea of what users must do.

**Examples**  
Sign up to receive our newsletter.  
Select the checkbox to agree to legal terms and conditions.  
To give consent for data collection or use, turn on the toggle.  
To unsubscribe, select the link.

If you must use the terms:

- Use *opt in* and *opt out* as verb phrases. Write *opt in to*, not *opt into*.
- Use the hyphenated forms *opt-in* and *opt-out* as adjectives. Don't use them as nouns.

**Examples**  
If you want to opt in to our offer program, follow the link.  
Complete the opt-out form.  
Opting out isn't an option.
