## real time, real-time

The term *real time* describes something that's diplayed or reported as an event is happening.

The noun form is two words and typically follows the preposition *in*. Hyphenate as an adjective that precedes a noun.

**Examples**  
Respond quickly to security events by monitoring them in real time.  
Respond quickly to security events by using real-time monitoring.
