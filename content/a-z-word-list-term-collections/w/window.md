## window

Don't use as a verb. In UI, a window is the main work area for an application. It typically includes controls for maximizing, resizing, and closing. [Dialog boxes](../d/dialog-box-dialog-dialogue) and [panes](../p/pane) can appear within a window, depending on the application.
