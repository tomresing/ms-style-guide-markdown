## tooltip

A tooltip is a small pop-up note that provides information about a UI element (such as a control, icon, or link) when a user hovers over it or selects it.

The term *tooltip* is one word and lowercase. Don't spell it as *ToolTip*.

The guidelines for creating tooltips vary by product. Here are general principles:

- Use them sparingly because they can be distracting.
- Be brief.
- Make sure that the information is helpful as a tip and doesn't just repeat what a label shows.
- Use sentence-style capitalization. (Capitalize only the first word and proper nouns.)
- If the tooltip is a fragment, don't end it with a period. If it needs to read like a full sentence, end it with a period.
