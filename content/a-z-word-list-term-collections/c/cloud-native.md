## cloud native, cloud-native

Cloud-native architecture and technologies are an approach to designing, constructing, and operating workloads that are built in the cloud and take full advantage of the cloud computing mode.

Guidelines:

- Lowercase.
- Hyphenate as an adjective that precedes a noun, as in *cloud-native app*. Don't hyphenate in other instances.
- In general, avoid *cloud native* and *cloud-native*. They might be unclear to some audiences or come across as jargon. Use friendlier language like *cloud-first*, *cloud-based*, *cloud-optimized*, *built for the cloud*, or *built in the cloud*.
- OK to use with developers and other technical audiences.
