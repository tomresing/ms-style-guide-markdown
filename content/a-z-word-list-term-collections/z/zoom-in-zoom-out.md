## zoom in, zoom out

Use *zoom in* and *zoom out* as generic verbs in instructions for all input methods.

Don't use *dezoom* or *unzoom.*

**See also** [Touch and pen interaction term collection](../term-collections/touch-pen-interaction-terms)
