## Plug and Play, plug and play, plug-and-play

Use *Plug and Play* as a noun or adjective to refer to the specifications developed by Intel for automatically configuring peripheral devices. Capitalize the term. Don't hyphenate it, including when it precedes a noun.

Use lowercase *plug and play* for the generic ability to automatically configure an added device. Hyphenate *plug-and-play* when it precedes and modifies a noun. When used as a noun or predicate adjective, write *plug and play* (no hyphens).

Don't use as a synonym for *Universal Plug and Play* (UPnP).

**Examples**  
Branded noun: Plug and Play records the information in an event log.  
Branded adjective: The printer supports Plug and Play functionality.  
Generic noun: The device supports plug and play.  
Generic adjective: Connect a plug-and-play printer.  
Predicate adjective: The printer is plug and play.
