## plugin

A plugin is a component that adds specific abilities to an application. In generative AI experiences, a plugin is a component that extends what a language model or an agent can do by giving it access to capabilities in Microsoft and non-Microsoft services and sites through APIs.

In either case, use one word (no hyphen).

Don't use as a synonym for *add-in* or *add-on*.
