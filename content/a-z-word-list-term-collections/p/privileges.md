## privileges

Privileges are elevated permissions that allow a user, group, or service principal to perform administrative or sensitive actions on a system or resource. Don't use as a synonym for *permissions* or *rights*.

**See also** [permissions](permissions), [rights](../r/rights), [user rights](../u/user-rights)
