## URI

A uniform resource identifier (URI) is a series of characters that identify a resource. A [URL](url) is a type of URI.

**Guidelines**

- In content for a general audience, use *resource ID* rather than *URI* unless you need to match the interface.
- In content for a technical audience, you don't need to spell out *URI* on the first mention.
- If you need to spell out the term, use lowercase.
- Use *a,* not *an,* as an article before *URI*.
- Use *of* (not *for*) to describe the relationship of the word *URI* to a resource.
