## lifecycle

One word. This style choice is an intentional deviation from the Merriam-Webster Dictionary.

**Examples**  
For products under existing lifecycle policies, we'll continue to provide support.  
New capabilities for the products are available across the entire customer lifecycle.  
With purpose-built agents, AI spans the full lifecycle of the contact center.
