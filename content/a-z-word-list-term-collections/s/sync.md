## sync

You can abbreviate *synchronize* or *synchronization* to *sync* for a friendlier tone, if it works well in the context. For example, a user can sync a file, folder, or library with a local drive, a SharePoint drive, a server, or the cloud. A user can also sync settings, messages, and other data across devices, apps, and accounts.

Abbreviations of *synchronize* and *synchronization* don't save space when they're localized. In languages other than English, localization uses the unabbreviated forms.

**Guidelines**

- Don't include *h* in the abbreviation. Use *sync*, *syncs*, *synced*, and *syncing*, not *synch*, *synchs*, *synched*, or *synching*.
- In general, use the phrase *sync with* instead of *sync from* or *sync to*. If information is being synced to and from two sources, describe it as syncing between or syncing across.
- Don't use *sync* to abbreviate *synchronous*.
