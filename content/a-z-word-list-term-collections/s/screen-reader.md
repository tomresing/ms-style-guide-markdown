## screen reader

A screen reader is assistive software that renders text and image content as speech or braille output.

When the term is a noun, it's two words. When you're using it as an adjective before a noun, hyphenate it for clarity. Never use a one-word form.

**Examples**  
The screen reader reads the contents of the screen aloud for users who are blind or have low vision.  
The app is designed for screen-reader compatibility.
